THE LEGACY

A kid from Calumet Park who took over the WRLD

Born December 2, 1998 outside Chicago, Jarad Higgins grew up playing piano, drums and guitar before he ever picked up a mic. "Juice WRLD" wasn't a random name — he said it stood for taking over the world, and between Goodbye & Good Riddance (2018), Death Race for Love (his first No. 1 album), and the collaborative WRLD ON DRUGS with Future, he pretty much did.

He was famous for freestyling entire songs on the spot — no pen, no notebook, just whatever was in his head, turned into a hook in one take. That gift is a huge part of why the catalog is so deep: hundreds of unreleased records fans still discover to this day.

In 2020, the posthumous album Legends Never Die debuted at No. 1 on the Billboard 200 — proof his music was only getting bigger. It's why this page exists: not to dwell on an ending, but to keep the actual music, in front of actual people, forever.
